2.Implicaciones y consecuencias

I tried to look for this subject in the real world. Obviously, they were not going to be there. This subject inhabits the liminal space between reality and the screen. They are a physical subject, yes, but one who lives through virtual performance. They have a body, but their being is digital.

I was told an anecdote about a free outdoor yoga class where, while the activity was taking place, people were busy recording content for their social media. They were not doing yoga; that was secondary. They were producing the image of someone who does yoga. The experience took place in physical space, but only to be transformed into content. If that is the case, then what is the purpose of physical space?

This reminded me of content creator farms: spaces where several people simultaneously produce images of themselves to feed the platforms. A kind of production line for identities. A lifestyle factory designed to sell courses, products, and, above all, the promise of a particular way of life.

Perhaps this is the subject I was looking for. A subject who does not need to possess what they aspire to, but rather demonstrate that they belong to it and sell that promise to others. An aspirational subject with a Labubu profession, whose identity is constructed through what they are able to display. A subject so deeply simulated that the only possible direction seems to be to simulate them even further.
