1.Tecnologías, materiales y decisiones formales

I have been searching for a subject produced by the technological and consumer systems of our time. I sought to simulate this subject in order to investigate a life that increasingly behaves like a simulation. The Sims 2, a relatively old game, far removed from the futuristic appearance we associate with technology and artificial intelligence, allowed me to represent a contemporary subject through an obsolete medium as a first approach. I sought to use deliberately broken animations within a wellness practice, where movement becomes physically impossible. The chroma key functioned as a constructed stage: the landscape participates in the construction of the lifestyle.

Broadcasting it as a live stream means introducing the work into the very structure I am investigating. It becomes content. The possibility that someone might enter becomes part of the experiment. The audio creates a dissonance between the promise of wellness and its materialization.

The limitations become important: pre-programmed bodies, broken animation without autonomy, and circulation through platforms that introduce their own rules and narratives.
