3.Red relacional

Some of these references seem to serve a dual function, such as Dancing Plague, Super Mario Clouds, or Pro Revolution Soccer. However, what they show me are different ways of intervening videogame software for artistic purposes, and even examples of how, without intervening them, one can create personal narratives within them as is the case with Grand Theft Hamlet.

Other works like Bunker for a Tropical Pixel and Morphecore bring me closer to this use of the digital glitch as a key part of the piece, while works like Follow the Sand are made up of the kind of handmade and cheesy aesthetic I am looking for. It remains a pending task to start exploring the installation side of the work.

On the theoretical side, Cute Accelerationism covers the core of the research at this point, but it is important to include a lot of Byung-Chul Han, and I have started exploring Foucault's concept of Biopolitics.
